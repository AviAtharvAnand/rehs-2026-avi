# Mesquite Recycling Guide

Source: Virgin Valley Disposal & Recycling  
Source page: https://vvdisposal.com/services-pricing/recycling/

## General Recycling Guidance

Virgin Valley Disposal provides recycling services for the Mesquite community.

Recyclables should be:

- Empty
- Clean
- Dry

## Aluminum and Metal Recycling

Recycle food and beverage metal cans, including:

- Steel cans
- Tin cans
- Bi-metal cans
- Aluminum cans

Accepted examples include:

- Aluminum beverage cans
- Food cans
- Scrap metal

## Paper Recycling

Accepted paper includes:

- Newspaper
- Envelopes
- Junk mail
- Phone books
- Brochures
- Magazines
- Office paper
- Greeting cards
- File folders

## Plastic Recycling

Recycle plastics marked #1 through #7.

Look on the bottom of containers for a number inside the recycling arrows.

Accepted examples include:

- Milk jugs, but not cartons
- Water bottles
- Soda containers
- Shampoo bottles
- Soap bottles
- Detergent bottles

## Cardboard Recycling

Greasy pizza boxes are not recyclable.

Accepted examples include:

- Ream wrappers
- File folders
- Poster board
- Frozen food boxes
- Cardboard boxes
- Milk cartons
- Newspaper inserts
- Catalogs
- Paperback books
- Phone books
- Brochures

## Items Not Accepted in Drop-Off or 35-Gallon Recycling Containers

- Appliances
- Batteries
- Bubble wrap
- Christmas lights
- Coat hangers
- Diapers
- Electrical cords
- Food waste
- Food wrap
- Garden hoses
- Plastic bags
- Polystyrene
- Rubber balls
- Sports equipment
- Stuffed animals
- Syringes
- Razor blades
- Tires
- Waxed cartons
- Wood
- Yard waste
- Glass

## Glass

Virgin Valley Disposal does not accept glass in its recycling program.

Reasons given include:

- Collected glass can be highly contaminated.
- Broken glass can contaminate paper and cardboard.
- Glass is heavy and costly to transport.
- Different glass types and colors are difficult to separate.
- Pyrex is not recyclable through this program.
- Window glass is generally not recyclable through this program.
- Broken glass can create safety hazards for workers.

Usable glass items may be suitable for reuse or upcycling.

## Drop-Off Recycling

Virgin Valley Disposal provides four recycling containers at two locations:

- Two containers in the Smith's parking lot
- Two containers in the Walmart parking lot

The drop-off program accepts four commodities:

- Aluminum cans
- Plastic milk jugs / plastic water bottles
- Newspaper
- Corrugated cardboard

Residents may use these containers free of charge.

Commercial customers should contact Virgin Valley Disposal to ask whether service is available.

The drop-off sites are open:

- 24 hours a day
- 7 days a week

Keep materials clean and use the designated openings to help prevent contamination.

## Curbside Recycling

The City of Mesquite and Virgin Valley Disposal operate a curbside recycling program.

Curbside recyclable materials listed in the source are:

- Aluminum cans
- Metal cans
- Newspaper

### First Wednesday of Each Month

Place aluminum and metal cans in the **gray container**.

### Third Wednesday of Each Month

Place newspaper in the **blue container**.

## Curbside Preparation

- Keep recyclable materials clean.
- Do not mix garbage or other materials into recycling containers.
- Recyclables should be clean, dry, and empty.
- Rinse recyclable items as needed to remove food or remaining contents.
