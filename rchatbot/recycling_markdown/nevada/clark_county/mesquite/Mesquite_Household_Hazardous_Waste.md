# Mesquite Household Hazardous Waste

Source: Virgin Valley Disposal & Recycling  
Source page: https://vvdisposal.com/services-pricing/hhw/

## What Is Household Hazardous Waste?

Household Hazardous Waste includes materials such as:

- Motor oil
- Paints
- Pesticides
- Household chemicals
- Fertilizers

These materials can be dangerous to people and the environment and should not be placed in regular trash receptacles.

To identify potentially hazardous products, check labels for words such as:

- Danger
- Oxidizer
- Warning
- Irritant
- Corrosive
- Harmful
- Flammable
- Fatal
- Explosive
- Poison

## Disposal Rules

Household Hazardous Waste should not be placed in garbage or recycling containers.

It must be taken to a licensed facility for treatment and recycling.

Do not:

- Put Household Hazardous Waste in trash
- Dump it into storm drains
- Dump it into sewer systems
- Dump it into septic systems
- Dump it on the ground or street

Improper disposal can endanger residents, refuse collectors, workers, pets, and the environment.

## Where Mesquite Residents Can Take Household Hazardous Waste

Residents can take Household Hazardous Waste to the City of Mesquite Landfill.

- Notify the gatekeeper about the hazardous waste.
- The waste will be stored in a secured area until it can be transported to a licensed facility.
- The service is free for residential customers on the 90-gallon system.

Commercial customers must take Household Hazardous Waste to a licensed facility.

## Ways to Reduce Household Hazardous Waste

- Buy only what you can use.
- Share leftovers with friends or neighbors when appropriate.
- Recycle used motor oil, transmission oil, antifreeze, car batteries, and paints.
- Buy safer alternatives to Household Hazardous Waste products.
- Follow label directions.
- Store unused products in a safe, dry, cool place.
