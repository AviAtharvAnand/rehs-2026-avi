# Mesquite Medical Waste Disposal

Source: Virgin Valley Disposal & Recycling  
Source page: https://vvdisposal.com/medical-waste-service/

## Medical Waste Service

Virgin Valley Disposal offers a medical waste program using a special box and bag that are delivered to the customer. When the container is full, the customer calls the office for pickup.

Medical waste collection is available from on-call service up to weekly collection.

## Scheduling

- Provide at least 24 hours' notice so service can be scheduled.
- Regular pickups are currently on Wednesdays.
- Pickups on other days are considered unscheduled and may involve extra fees.

## Packaging Requirements

- Place all medical waste in a government-approved biohazard bag.
- The biohazard bag must be properly tied and secured.
- Place the secured biohazard bag inside the corrugated cardboard box supplied by Virgin Valley Disposal.
- Close the box lids properly.
- Virgin Valley Disposal will only remove medical waste that is properly closed and secured.
- Replacement boxes and bags are left in quantities equal to those removed.
- Only boxes and bags supplied by the contractor will be serviced.

## Sharps

- All sharps must be placed in a government-approved sharps container suitable for carrying them.
- Place the sharps container inside the government-approved medical waste bag.
- Place that bag inside the supplied medical waste box.

## Container Size

The medical waste program is intended for large and small companies as well as residents who need the service.

Approximate container details:

- Average box size: 14 x 17 x 25 inches
- Bag capacity: 33 gallons

Measurements are approximate outside dimensions and may vary by manufacturer.
