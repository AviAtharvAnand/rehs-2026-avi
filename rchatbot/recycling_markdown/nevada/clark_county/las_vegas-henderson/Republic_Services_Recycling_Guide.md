# Republic Services Recycling Guide

Source: Republic Services - "What's Recyclable?"

## Acceptable Items

### Plastics
Recycle plastics marked #1, #2, and #5. Look on the bottom of containers for the number inside the recycling arrows.

### Metal Cans
Recycle all food and beverage metal cans, including steel, tin, and aluminum.

### Cardboard, Newspaper & Magazines
Recycle newspaper inserts, catalogs, paperback books, phone books, brochures, cardboard, newspapers, and magazines.

### Paper Food Containers & Cartons
Recycle items such as egg cartons (plastic or cardboard), cereal boxes, and pasta boxes.

### Paper
Recycle envelopes, office paper, junk mail, greeting cards, and file folders.

### Glass
Recycle clear, brown, and green bottles and jars.

## Preparation Rules

- Do not bag recyclables; place them loose in the recycling cart.
- Recyclables should be empty, clean, and dry before being placed in the recycling cart.
