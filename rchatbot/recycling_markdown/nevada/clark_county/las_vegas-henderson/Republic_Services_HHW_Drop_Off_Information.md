# Republic Services Household Hazardous Waste Drop-Off Information

Source: Republic Services, 2024 Household Hazardous Waste Calendar  
Website shown in source: www.republicservicesvegas.com

## Acceptable Household Hazardous Waste

Items must be in structurally sound and sealed containers.

- Paint
- Oil / gasoline
  - Gasoline limit: 5 gallons
- Batteries
- Pesticide / fertilizer
- Flammable liquids
- Aerosol cans
- Drain cleaners
- Oven cleaners
- Adhesives
- Pool chemicals

For disposal information about items not listed, the source directs residents to the Southern Nevada Health District or Earth911.

## Household Waste Limits

- Household waste only
- Maximum 40 pounds of dry material
- Maximum 15 gallons of liquid, measured by container size
- Gasoline is restricted to 5 gallons

## Drop-Off Hours

Household hazardous waste drop-off is open:

- Wednesday through Saturday
- 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM
- Closed on holidays
- Service rotates between the North Valley and South Valley locations according to the calendar schedule

## North Valley Location

**333 W Gowan Road**  
North Las Vegas, NV 89032

- Entrance on Gowan Road through Gate B only
- Recycling is also accepted according to the posted schedule

## South Valley Location

**560 Cape Horn Drive**  
Henderson, NV 89011

- Recycling is accepted Monday through Sunday
- Recycling hours: 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM
